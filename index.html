QUESTIONS & HELPDESK

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about enrollment, syllabus, and technical help.

How do I enroll in a course?
Simply click the "Enroll Now" button on any featured course card or register an account. Once registered, the course is immediately added to your interactive Student Dashboard where you can track lessons.
How do certificates work?
Upon finishing all modules and earning a score of 70% or higher on the final exam challenges, a secure, shareable digital certificate is automatically generated. You can attach it to your CV or share it directly on LinkedIn.
Are courses free?
EduSphere offers a broad library of free coursework across core subject disciplines. Advanced tracks, special live panels, and verified accreditation degrees may require premium tier tokens or university sponsorship.
How do live classes work?
Live streams are broadcast directly on our WebRTC platforms. Users can see upcoming streams via countdown timers, set notifications, participate in interactive boards, and communicate with tutors via active live chats.
